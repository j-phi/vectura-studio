### TUX-001: phone header and hamburger menu

- In phone layout (the TUX-006 `phone-layout` classifier, not a fixed window width), the menubar collapses into a hamburger sheet.
- Undo, Redo and Export are direct 44 px header buttons. The sheet drills down one menu at a time and scrolls when a menu is taller than the viewport.
- The modifier bar shows glyphs (shift, alt, cmd, ctrl, pan) in every phone viewport, including iPhone landscape.
- The phone header stays below an open modal, and works again after the modal closes.
- A mouse window never scrolls horizontally between 500 and 640 px. Desktop shows no hamburger and no Theme row.
