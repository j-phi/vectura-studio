# TUX-014: tool sub-menus open by tap and stay open

## CHANGELOG
- Touch: tap the active tool again, or press and hold it (0.28 s; 0.4 s for Draw Algorithm), to open its variants. The menu stays open when you lift your finger. Tap a row to pick it, tap the tool again to close, or tap anywhere else to close. An outside tap only closes the menu: it does not draw, select or change the tool.
- Touch: a first tap on an inactive variant tool (Shape, Pen, Fill, Scissor, Draw Algorithm) activates its last variant, as before. Moving 10 px, or a system cancel, stops the hold.
- Touch: sub-menu rows show a text label and are at least 44 px. The menu opens beside the button, clamped to the visual viewport, and scrolls inside when it is taller than the space (the Draw Algorithm list on a phone in landscape). No text selection or iOS callout on the rail buttons or rows.
- Mouse and pen-less desktop behaviour is unchanged: click activates, hold 0.28 s opens, release in place closes, drag onto a row selects, rows stay 28 px icons.
- New `Vectura.UI.ToolPopover = { place, bindTouchDismiss }` (in `src/ui/shell/toolbar.js`) for the next popover lanes. New `app.ui.setToolbarDock(side)`. New config `UI_CONSTANTS.TOOL_SUBMENU`.
