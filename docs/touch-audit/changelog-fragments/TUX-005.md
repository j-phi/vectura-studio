# TUX-005 Safe-area insets on the right elements

## (a) CHANGELOG bullet
- Touch: safe-area insets now land on the right elements. `tokens.css` defines `--safe-top/right/bottom/left` (the only `env(safe-area-inset-*)` in the skin). The header grows by the top inset. The toolbar no longer carries it (no more band under the header). The bottom inset sits on the bottom-most visible chrome (`#hud-bar`, else `#touch-modifier-bar`). Drawers end above that chrome (`--bottom-chrome-h`, measured). Landscape drawers keep their content out of the notch. The phone header is now 44 px tall so the 44 px action buttons stay inside it.

## Fix round: closed Document Setup drawer in the iPhone landscape inset band

- The closed phone `#settings-panel` is `position: fixed; left: var(--safe-left)`. Its base `translateX(-100%)` left the right edge at 62 px, so the drawer and its close button painted in the left band.
- Fix: `body.phone-layout #settings-panel:not(.open)` shifts by `-100% - var(--safe-left)` and is `visibility: hidden`. The open state is unchanged ([62,0,812,381]).
- Test J in `tests/e2e/touch/safe-area.spec.js` covers every closed `.settings-panel` on the profiles with side insets.

## Fix round 3: floating rail overlapped the bottom chrome on iPad landscape

- Cause: `clampFloat()` and the default centring in `src/ui/shell/toolbar.js` used the `.workspace-shell` rect. With the taller touch chrome and the t24/b20 insets, the rail ended at 778 px while `#viewport-container` ended at 745 px. The bottom rail controls then sat under `.bottom-pane-header`.
- Fix: new `floatBounds()` limits the rail to the `#viewport-container` rect, expressed in shell coordinates. Both `clampFloat()` and the default Y centring use it.
- The footer (pin, home, rotate) is `display: none` under `(pointer: coarse)` since TUX-016, so the pin tap check applies only where the pin is visible.
- Test K (iPad landscape, both engines, 1210x834 and 1180x820, initial and after Home) asserts rail bounds and bottom-control reachability. Test J now checks the closed drawer rect without the visibility skip and without the dead `elementFromPoint` probe.
