# TUX-033 — Panels and renderer honour touch modifiers

## CHANGELOG bullet
- The layer list now reads modifiers through `Vectura.InputModifiers.readModifiers`. The Multi-select task toggles rows in and out by touch. Real Shift (range) and Cmd/Ctrl (toggle) are unchanged. A click reads the task snapshot taken at the last touch `pointerdown` (up to 1 s old). This survives the one-shot release at `pointerup` and WebKit's `pointerType: 'mouse'` on the click after a tap. A mouse `pointerdown` clears the snapshot.
- Fill sample (Cmd/Ctrl-click on a fill) is available by touch through the Multi-select task. Drag-pour and include-occluded read through `renderer.getModifierState`.
- `#layer-list` is a consuming surface for the one-shot latch (`src/config/touch-modifiers.js`).

## plans.md line
TUX-033 landed: layer list and fill sample read touch modifier tasks through InputModifiers.

## README / help note
In the layer list, the Multi-select button adds or removes the next tapped layer. Double-tap it to keep it on. On the Fill tool, Multi-select plus a tap samples the fill under the finger.

## Decisions
- Multi-select in the layer list = toggle-add (Cmd). Range select (Shift) stays keyboard-only on touch.
- Fill sample on touch = Multi-select task (Cmd/Ctrl was removed from the bar by TUX-045).
- Drag-pour reads `getModifierState(e).shift` (the Constrain task). Touch and pen are deferred-pour (TUX-022), so this path stays mouse-only in practice.

## Guard rule text for TUX-060
"Raw `e.shiftKey|metaKey|ctrlKey|altKey` in a pointer or click handler outside `src/ui/input-modifiers.js` is a violation." Exempt: keydown/keyup, wheel, HTML5 drag events (dragstart/dragover/drop; TUX-044). Baseline must not rise.
