# TUX-024 fragment

## CHANGELOG bullet
- Touch: the pen and Direct tools use finger-sized targets. A finger grabs an anchor, a Bezier handle, a corner widget or the light within 22 px, closes a pen path with a tap within 22 px of the start point, and finishes it with a double-tap (24 px, 400 ms, measured with `event.timeStamp`). The nearest target wins, and a tie goes to the anchor. A pen tap with under 6 px of finger jitter makes no handles. A second tap right after a close does not start a new path. Mouse and Apple Pencil keep their old sizes (6, 5, 7, 5 and 8 px). Config: `UI_CONSTANTS.HIT_TOLERANCE` and `Vectura.hitTolerance(kind, pointerType)`, plus `Renderer._isDoubleTap`.
- Fix (all pointers): a pen double-click or double-tap that finishes an open path no longer leaves a duplicate anchor on top of the last one.

## Notes for the integrator
- The Select tool corner widget uses the 22 px touch radius. It shares the corner with the TUX-023 resize zone, so the nearer centre wins (`Renderer._nearestResizeCentreDistance`). A tie goes to the widget. Mouse and pen keep the 7 px radius.
- The light hit test runs first in `down()` for every tool. On touch it now has a 22 px radius. For the Direct tool the nearest target wins.
- Out of scope, untouched: mirror-guide hits (TUX-027), Scene 3D double-tap (TUX-025), Type-tool multi-click, `mergeTol`, geometry `snapTol`, algo-draw tap.

## Fix round
- Pen close-drag slop and handle are measured from the touch-down point, not from the start anchor. A jittered finger close tap 10-20 px off the start now closes with no in-handle on anchor 0. For every pointer type, the close-drag handle now follows the drag delta from the press point, and a move under the slop makes no handle. Shift snaps that delta to 45 degree steps.
