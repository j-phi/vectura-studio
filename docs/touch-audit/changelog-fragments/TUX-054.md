# TUX-054: Downloads and export save on iOS

## CHANGELOG
- Fixed Save, Export SVG, Export Animated SVG, preset bundle and designer exports on iOS. A shared `Vectura.Download.saveBlob` helper now opens the share sheet ("Save to Files") on touch devices that can share files. It keeps the anchor download elsewhere and revokes the blob URL 30 s after the click. The success toast now appears only after the file is saved, never after a cancelled share sheet.
