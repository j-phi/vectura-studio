## CHANGELOG bullet
- **Touch: 44px targets on the layers pane, left pane, pens rows and modifier bar.** `tokens.css` adds `--touch-target-min: 44px` and `--touch-target-floor: 24px`. A `TUX-041` block at the end of `components.css`, keyed only on `(pointer: coarse)`, sizes section headers, selects, preset triggers, value chips, noise-rack controls, layers header controls, draw-order icons and modifier buttons. Small visuals (info buttons, noise grip, toggles, draw-order icons, pen remove) keep their size and gain a centred 44px `::before`. Pens rows become two finger-tall lines (`[grip][name][remove]`, `[swatch][weight][value]`); the round pen icon is hidden on coarse because the swatch shows the colour. On short landscape phones the layers list keeps room for two rows and the Pens tab scrolls. Desktop is unchanged.

## Layout fixes found on the way
- Pen rows were crushed to 0px name width at pane width 211 (grip covered). Fixed by the two-line layout.
- `.pen-item` shrank below its content on short viewports (`flex-shrink:0` now).
- The modifier bar covered the last control of the left pane on iPad portrait (`#left-panel-content` bottom padding).
- WebKit ignored `min-height` on one native select, so selects also get an explicit `height`.
- A classic scrollbar on `#layer-list` stole the right 6px of the row's last button in WebKit (hidden on coarse).
