# TUX-015 All Tools drawer fits and is finger-sized

## CHANGELOG bullet
- Touch: the All Tools drawer now opens inside the visible viewport on every phone and tablet. It places itself under a horizontal rail, beside a vertical rail, or over the top edge when there is no room below. It clamps to the visual viewport (not `innerWidth`) and to the safe-area insets. It re-places on visual-viewport resize and scroll. Its body scrolls inside the viewport (`overscroll-behavior: contain`). It stacks above the rail, the panes and the modifier bar (`--z-tool-drawer: 80`, was 45). On coarse pointers, grid items are 44x44, list rows are 44 px tall and the grid/list toggles are 44x44. Mouse sizes (34x34, 24x22) are unchanged. Placement is one pure function, `Vectura.UI.ToolDrawer.computeDrawerPlacement`.
