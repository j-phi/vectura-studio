# TUX-043 fragment: sliders are finger-sized; scrolling and tapping never change a value

## CHANGELOG bullet
- **Touch: sliders are 44px tall with a 28px thumb, and only a sideways drag changes a value.** On coarse pointers every `<input type="range">` gets a 44px box, a 4px track (pen 3px, draw-order 6px) and a 28px centred thumb (`--touch-slider-thumb`), with `touch-action: pan-y` (`pan-x` on the vertical Text-panel slider). One delegated controller, `Vectura.UI.RangeTouch` in `slider.js`, handles touch and pen pointers on all ranges: a vertical swipe scrolls the panel, a tap on the track does nothing, and a sideways drag from the thumb or the band moves the value relatively (1:1 with the finger), with one `change` on release. `UI.Slider` dual ranges (`.is-dual-min`/`.is-dual-max`) move the thumb nearest the touch. Mouse and trackpad keep the native track-jump and the double-click reset.

## Decisions / notes
- Q7: draw-order tap-to-play is kept. A tap plays from the current handle position; the handle no longer jumps to the tap point on touch. The controller never stops pointer events, so `shortcuts.js` needs no change.
- Pointer policy: `touch` and `pen` are controlled. `mouse` is never touched (native track-jump and `dblclick` reset stay, also on an iPad trackpad).
- Native browser `input`/`change` events (trusted) are restored and swallowed at window capture during a touch gesture and for 500 ms after it. Untrusted programmatic events pass, so undo and `setValue` are not blocked.
- Gap: exact entry on legacy `attachValueEditor`-span sliders (algo-config-panel) and `.pen-width-value` arrives with TUX-038.
- Pen rows (TUX-041 two-line layout) leave the pen slider only about 55px wide on iPhone portrait. The control works, but a wider slider needs a layout call in the pens panel.
- J1 slider-step cell: not flipped. It cannot strict-PASS on iPhone until TUX-010 lands.
- D4: this branch has no `tests/e2e/touch/target-baseline.json`, so no baseline was lowered. `targets-controls.spec.js` still excludes ranges; lane H can include them now (the input box is 44px).

- Known gap: nearest-thumb selection works only for `UI.Slider` duals (`.is-dual-min`/`.is-dual-max`). The legacy algo-config `.dual-range` (`rangeDual` params) and the harmonograph plot range keep the native top-input behaviour: the topmost input owns the drag, so a thumb under it cannot be picked. Fix by routing them through `UI.Slider` dual (lane G / Phase 3).
- Draw-order footer on iPhone landscape (874x402): the pane reserved 56px for the modifier bar, but the bar is 57px and the HUD strip adds 27px, so the pane ran 28px under the bar and the bar covered the slider track (`elementFromPoint` hit `.touch-modifier-bar`; a drag left the value at 100). The TUX-043 block now reserves 84px at the pane bottom (coarse, wider than 640px). On short coarse viewports (height 520px or less) it also hides the idle layer status strip (`#layer-status-bar`, 32px) and tightens the draw-order band (14px top padding keeps the 44px eye/gear hit boxes off the last layer row), so the 44px slider sits at y 271-315 above the bar without scrolling. Trade-off: the idle layer status strip is not shown on short phone landscape. A sticky or re-ordered footer was tried and rejected: it covered layer rows and broke the TUX-012 and TUX-041 hit-area specs.
- Known limit: iPhone landscape with browser chrome (874x330) has a 172px pane scroller, less than the layer list plus the slider, so the draw-order slider there needs a pane scroll. `sliders.spec.js` (a) allows the scroll rescue only on that viewport and on tall ones.
