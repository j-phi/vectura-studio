# TUX-051 - Bottom pane no longer expands on a stray finger tap

## CHANGELOG bullet
- **Bottom pane touch fix (TUX-051):** a finger tap near the canvas bottom edge no longer expands the collapsed bottom pane or saves that state. On touch and pen, the divider (`#bottom-resizer`) expands the pane only after a 6 px drag (`UI_CONSTANTS.TOUCH_GESTURE.touchDragSlopPx`); a press that began outside the divider (WebKit retarget) is ignored. Mouse keeps expand-on-press. On coarse pointers the collapsed pane is 52 px tall and its chevron is a 44 x 44 target (`--touch-bottom-collapsed-h`).
