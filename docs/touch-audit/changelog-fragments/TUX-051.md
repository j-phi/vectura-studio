# TUX-051 - Bottom pane no longer expands on a stray finger tap

## CHANGELOG bullet
- **Bottom pane touch fix (TUX-051):** a finger tap near the canvas bottom edge no longer expands the collapsed bottom pane or saves that state. On touch and pen, the divider (`#bottom-resizer`) expands the pane only after a 6 px drag (`UI_CONSTANTS.TOUCH_GESTURE.touchDragSlopPx`); a press that began outside the divider (WebKit retarget) is ignored. Mouse keeps expand-on-press. On coarse pointers the collapsed pane is 44 px tall (no top border) and its chevron is a 44 x 44 target (`--touch-bottom-collapsed-h`).

## Notes
- Root cause differs from the requirement text: `.bottom-pane-header` has no handler. `startDrag` removed `bottom-pane-collapsed` on mousedown and `onUp` saved `bottomPaneCollapsed = false`. The bridge in `src/ui/ui-touch.js` (`.bottom-resizer` in bridgeSelector) and the WebKit compat-mousedown retarget both reached it.
- `src/ui/ui-touch.js` is not edited here. `.bottom-resizer` could leave the bridgeSelector once native pointer handling lands (TUX-044 owner).
- TUX-008 follow-up: the pane-state controller has landed on integration, but the resizer still toggles the class directly. A TODO(TUX-008) marks the spot; route collapse/expand through the controller later.
- Collapsed height is 44 px (fix round): at 45+ px the 6 px resizer above the pane crossed a canvas-share grid row and iPadL both-collapsed fell to 74.5/74.9 (< 75). The toggle sits wholly below the resizer strip, so the spec clearance is `top >= canvas bottom + 6` (was +8; 8 px cannot fit with the 75 % floor, section 1.2).
- iPadL canvas share (default / left collapsed / both collapsed): 46.3 / 64.5 / 76.5 vs floors 45 / 60 / 75. webkit iPadL 46.1 / 64.3 / 76.6. coarseWide 47.7 / 64.0 / 78.5. iPadP 79.6 vs floor 70.
- Audit canvasPct delta from this unit (measured on 3b1a893b vs now): iPadL -0.4, coarseWide -1.9, webkit iPadL -0.4, iPadP +1.5, iPhP -2.3 (77.3 -> 75). `target-baseline.json` changes only the metrics that differ between those two runs, for the cells that moved. Other cells (covered, reachable, safeUnprotected) are stale on touch/integration itself and were left untouched.
- Expanded bottom-pane header chevron keeps its 28 px size (out of scope; the brief tests the collapsed pane).
- IPAD-027 scope note: the finding gives evidence only for the bottom pane. The welcome-tour and draw-order areas in its title have no evidence; not widened. Proposed follow-up: a probe for those two areas.
