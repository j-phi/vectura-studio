# TUX-039: Icon buttons name themselves on touch

## CHANGELOG
- Touch: hold any icon-only button for 0.5 s to see its name. The tip shows beside the button while the finger is down and stays 1.5 s after the lift. Lifting does not activate the button. A quick tap still activates it. Mouse and pen are unchanged (the native `title` still shows on hover). Variant tools, the draw-algorithm button and drag handles keep their own hold behaviour. Petal Designer subtool buttons and the layer filter button now have accessible names.
