# TUX-022 Paint bucket preview and scope by touch

## CHANGELOG bullet
- Paint bucket on touch and pen: press shows the region preview and a loupe above the finger, lift pours into the last previewed region. A cancel, a drag off the paper or a second finger pours nothing. Scope ▲/▼ chips (44 px, context bar, coarse pointers only) widen and narrow the previewed region. Mouse behaviour is unchanged.

## Deviations from the brief
- TUX-017 had already landed `_deferTouch(pour, 'window')` for the fill pour. TUX-022 removes that call for fill. Pinch (f) and cancel (d) already passed on this branch before the fix. The failing REDs were (a) loupe, (c), (e), (g), (i), (j).
- CODE-017 / req. 6 is done here (fix round): while a touch or pen holds the Fill tool, the move branch runs `_updateHoverFeedback` and `_updateSceneHoverHint` for the held pointer, before commit. `_clearFillDeferred` clears that feedback on lift, cancel and drag-off. Note: those two functions produce chips, labels and the 3D hint only for the Select and Direct tools, so with the Fill tool a mouse also sees none. The e2e test (k) therefore asserts that the feedback runs for the held pointer (spy) and that nothing lingers after lift or cancel. No new Fill-tool chip was invented.
- KNOWN LIMITATION (for Jay): the fill bar anchors to the bottom of the canvas (`#viewport-container`), not the viewport, so it can cover part of the canvas. Measured on iPhone landscape: bar y=226-278 inside canvas y=90-286, which covers 52 of 196 px (26%) of the canvas height. The chips stay at least 44 px, stay in the viewport and can be tapped (test g). Two-finger pan reaches the covered region. On iPhone landscape the loupe can also cover the right chip while the finger is near the bottom of the short canvas. Repositioning is not done in this lane.
- Scope chips step on `pointerdown` for touch and pen (and on `click` for mouse and keyboard). Reason: the browser sends no `click` for a second-finger tap while another finger holds the canvas. The follow-up click is ignored. Test (g2) covers the two-handed flow.
- Shift drag-pour is now mouse-only. A touch or pen hold with `shiftKey` no longer pours per region and no longer duplicates the lift pour. Test (l).
- `tests/e2e/paint-bucket-mouse.spec.js` is in the `test:e2e` chain (desktop-chromium). Its evidence screenshot runs only with `TUX_EVIDENCE=1`.

- Scope chips (second fix round): a touch or pen press steps on `pointerdown` and the click that follows is always ignored, whatever the press length (before: a 700 ms timer cleared the guard, so a 900 ms press stepped twice). The flag clears on that click, a mouse press or a key press. Test (m).
- FOR JAY TO CONFIRM OR OVERRIDE: for the Fill tool, Q11 "commit the first action" is read as "the hold is only a preview, so nothing commits". A late second finger (hold >= 400 ms, then pinch) pours nothing, leaves history unchanged and zooms. Before this lane, `_deferTouch(pour,'window')` poured at window close. Test (n) pins this.
- The section 3 RED path `tests/integration/touch-paint-bucket.test.js` was replaced by `tests/e2e/touch/paint-bucket.spec.js` (touch behavior) and `tests/integration/paint-bucket-pointer-parity.test.js` (mouse parity).

- [DEVICE] No iOS callout or magnifier shows during a fill hold.

- Scope chips (third fix round): a touch or pen press that is cancelled (`pointercancel`) steps back, so the offset is unchanged. The click guard expires 120 ms after `pointerup`, so a press without a click (second-finger tap) cannot swallow a later click (VoiceOver, programmatic). Tests (o) and (p).
- Fill bar vs canvas HUD (third fix round): on iPhone portrait the HUD Zoom In button (z 40) covered the Scope down chip (z 35) after the first pour, so tests (g) failed with the offset stuck. While the fill bar is mounted, the HUD now rides 60 px higher (`:has(.ctxbar--fill)` in components.css).
