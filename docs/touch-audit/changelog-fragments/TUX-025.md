# TUX-025 fragment

## CHANGELOG bullet
- Touch: the 3D rotation gizmo (scene orbit pad and the pseudo-3D layers) has a larger, separated layout when `body.touch-ui` or a coarse pointer is active. The pitch track sits in a vertical strip outside the roll ring, so the yaw and pitch markers no longer overlap (they were 1.7 px apart at yaw 270). Markers stay at least 28 px apart. A finger grabs a marker within 22 px, and the nearest target wins. Touch drags keep the grab offset, so the value does not jump on the first move. The gizmo shifts to stay inside the canvas, clear of floating canvas chrome (touch modifier bar, layer context bar, canvas HUD; `GIZMO_3D_TOUCH.occluders`), and mirrors the pitch track when the right edge clips. A touch hit that exists only because of the larger radius yields to the scene handles (light, shadow, object gizmo, resize, face-pull). Mouse and Apple Pencil keep the old geometry and 9 px radii. Config: `UI_CONSTANTS.GIZMO_3D_TOUCH` and the `gizmo3d-marker`, `gizmo3d-centre` and `gizmo3d-ring-band` rows of `HIT_TOLERANCE`.

## Notes for the integrator (requirement-doc corrections)
- The IPH-012 "orbit centre 5 px" citation ignores the 24 px pad fallback (padRadius + 7). The touch orbit zone is the 10 px centre zone plus that fallback.
- Add the edge TUX-023 -> TUX-025 to section 5.3 (shared `HIT_TOLERANCE` table, `Vectura.hitTolerance`).
- Light handles (`hitSceneLightGizmo`, `hitSceneLight`, `_hitSceneShadow`) belong to TUX-024. This unit does not edit them.
- Brief deviations (written reasons): the roll ring is 68 px (not 60) and the pitch track is at 98 px (not 90). With 60/90 the "18 px away" sample on the yaw marker fell nearer to the roll handle than to the yaw marker. The new values make the nearest-wins contract hold in every grid state. The clamp also keeps the footprint plus 22 px reach clear of `#touch-modifier-bar`, `.ctxbar` and `#canvas-hud`. On iPhone landscape the layer context bar covered the roll handle of a selected polyhedron (fix round).
- Test note: in this checkout the vitest config bundle reads the main worktree's conflicted package.json. Use `--configLoader runner` until that merge is resolved.
- The e2e first-step assertions use one tenth of the expected total plus slack, not 3 degrees. A 2 px first step of a 20 px pitch drag legitimately moves 6 degrees on the scene span.
