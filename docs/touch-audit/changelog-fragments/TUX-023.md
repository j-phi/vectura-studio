# TUX-023 fragment

## CHANGELOG bullet
- Touch: canvas transform handles use pointer-type hit radii. A finger grabs a resize corner within 22 px and rotates from a 48 px band. Mouse and Apple Pencil keep 10 px and 28 px. Handles draw at 12 px on touch (6 px otherwise), a round rotate handle shows beside the selection, and a selection narrower than 66 px keeps its edge handles off and nudges its corners outward so it still moves from its centre. Config: `UI_CONSTANTS.HIT_TOLERANCE` and `Vectura.hitTolerance(kind, pointerType)`.

## Fix round notes
- Rotate affordance offset (34) and disc radius (12) now live in UI_CONSTANTS.HIT_TOLERANCE (rotateAffordanceOffset, rotateAffordanceDraw).
- Affordance skips a corner when document.elementFromPoint at the disc centre or +/- r is not the canvas (docked chrome).
- Pointer-type flip between touch and mouse/pen redraws, so drawn handle size follows hit size.
- J3 rotate start moved to ne+(24,-24), inside the 22..48 px touch rotate band.
- Device row added as DEV-36 in docs/touch-audit/device-checklist.md (status open = pending).

## Fix round 2 notes
- Resize scale is measured from the grab point (`handleGrabOffset`), so a nudged corner zone does not jump on the first move (32 px selection, +8 drag: grew 25 px before, 8 px after).
- Touch ctxbar docks clear of the nudged resize zones: `Renderer.getHandleClearancePx()` plus the 12 px grip extender feeds the ctxbar anchor offset.
- Rotate affordance: fallback clamps inside the canvas; result cached 120 ms per geometry.
- J3 ledger entries removed (pass on all 8 primary projects). J3 rotate starts on the visible affordance (`getRotateAffordance`).
- Device row is DEV-42 after the merge with touch/integration.
- Overlap note for the integrator: renderer.js resize branch (`handleGrabOffset`) sits beside TUX-007 scale-clamp code.

## Round 4 additions
- Fix: on touch, a press at the centre of a tiny selection (about 14 px or less) now moves it. The true-corner fallback zone is skipped when the 10 px corner zones overlap.
- Fix: the touch rotate handle is re-anchored when the context bar shows, hides or moves, so it is never painted under the bar.

## Fix round 5 notes
- Thin selections (one axis under 66 px): on touch, the edge handles (n/s or e/w) now move outward along their normal, like the corners. Their zones no longer cover the centre, so a finger drag from the centre moves the object. Mouse and Apple Pencil results do not change.
- A true corner far from the centre (a long, thin selection) stays grabbable on touch.
- Rotate-affordance refresh is coalesced: the renderer invalidates its cache and repaints once on the next frame, only if no draw already ran. The context bar keeps one 350 ms settle timer, and it skips all layout reads on desktop.
- `renderer._rotAffDrawn` clears on every frame that paints no affordance (deselect, mouse).
