## Deviations (written reasons)
- Tablet handles stay in the header band (TUX-008 placed them there, above the rail, uncovered). The brief asked for a canvas top-band position; that band is covered by the rail's own controls and the canvas. The test asserts: not over the canvas, not over any rail control or drag handle.
- Right pane floor is 258 (skin manifest) on drawers and 200 on touch-wide (TUX-008 cap), not 260.
- Document Setup is 3 taps on phone (hamburger, File, Document Setup), 2 on tablet and touch-wide (adjudicated).
- Handle width is 48, not 44: the 2 px hit probe reads a 44 px box as 43.
- Z token `--z-drawer-handle 68` is defined but the handles stay at `--z-pane-opener` inside the header, so a tap on the other handle swaps drawers.
