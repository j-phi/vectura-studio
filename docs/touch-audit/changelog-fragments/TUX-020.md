# TUX-020 - Undo, Redo and zoom by finger

## CHANGELOG bullet
- Touch: add a canvas HUD with Undo, Redo, Zoom Out, Zoom In and Fit (46 px targets, bottom-right, safe-area aware). On phones the header Undo and Redo serve history. A two-finger tap undoes. A three-finger tap redoes. Pinch, wheel and the HUD share one zoom range (Vectura.VIEW minScale/maxScale). The pinch ceiling rises from 20 to 1000. Undo and Redo show a disabled state when the stack is empty. A resize no longer resets a user zoom.

## plans.md line
- [x] TUX-020 touch history and zoom controls: canvas HUD, two-/three-finger tap, shared zoom clamp, history availability event (`vectura:history`).

## README feature text
Touch devices get an on-canvas control strip (Undo, Redo, Zoom, Fit). Tap with two fingers to undo. Tap with three fingers to redo.

## Help text
"Two fingers tap the canvas to undo. Three fingers tap to redo. Use the strip at the bottom right to zoom in, zoom out or fit the page."

## Shortcut-list rows
| Gesture | Action |
|---|---|
| Two-finger tap | Undo |
| Three-finger tap | Redo |
| HUD buttons (touch only) | Undo, Redo, Zoom Out, Zoom In, Fit |
