## Findings from the measurement
- NEW-TOUCH-MOVE-HISTORY: on every touch project a quick one-finger drag that moves a selected shape does not push a history entry (history 1 -> 1), while a mouse drag does (1 -> 2) and a handle resize by touch does (+1). Undo cannot revert a touch move. J3 and J4 fail at `j3.move` / pass-through on this. Candidate for TUX-019 or TUX-020.
- iPhone landscape: all 36 cells (Chromium, WebKit, chrome variant) fail at `boot` with `renderer.scale` -0.074 (TUX-007).
- WebKit iPhP runs `body.phone-layout` (innerWidth 402); Chromium iPhP does not (544). Both fail J1 at `j1.open-layers`, covered by `.toolbar-drag-handle`.
- JRN-010 (rename on a heavy document) reproduces in Chromium only. WebKit passes J2 on iPad because synthetic taps do not queue behind the long task.
- Passing cells today: J8 (most projects), J9 and J10 on iPad, WebKit J2 on iPad.
